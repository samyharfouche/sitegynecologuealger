The practice

A practice focused on listening and trust.

Dr Mouhoub Dahbia's practice supports patients at every stage of their life: gynecological follow-up, pregnancy, ultrasound, infertility, screening, gynecological and breast conditions.

Located in Mohammadia, Algiers, opposite the town hall, the practice favors a human-centered approach, unhurried consultations and personalized medical care for each patient.

Pregnancy

Pregnancy follow-up & high-definition ultrasound.

The practice supports patients throughout their pregnancy with attentive follow-up, tailored examinations and ultrasounds to monitor the development of the fetus with complete peace of mind.

  • Regular follow-up consultations scheduled each trimester
  • 2D / 3D / 4D Ultrasound high-definition imaging
  • Fetal heart monitoring continuous heart rate recording
  • Personalized support attentive listening and availability
Gynecological follow-up

Prevention, screening and advice at every age.

Gynecological follow-up ensures prevention, screening, advice and management of gynecological conditions at every stage of a woman's life.

  • Contraception advice tailored to each profile
  • Screening cervix, breast, infections
  • Cycle disorders diagnosis and treatment
  • Pain & infections personalized care
  • Prevention cervical and breast cancers
Sterility & infertility

A discreet, caring journey for couples.

The practice carries out infertility assessments, provides an initial diagnosis and guides patients and couples toward the solutions best suited to their situation — with listening, patience and full confidentiality.

  • Complete assessment hormonal and morphological exploration
  • Personalized diagnosis at the couple's pace
  • Referral to specialized care providers
  • Confidentiality and support at every step
